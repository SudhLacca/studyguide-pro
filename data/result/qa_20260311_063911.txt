Q: what is document forgery research

Detection of image tampering: This involves analyzing an image to determine whether it has been altered or manipulated in any way.</div><div style='margin-left: 25px; margin-bottom: 12px; padding-left: 10px; border-left: 3px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.4);'>2.
