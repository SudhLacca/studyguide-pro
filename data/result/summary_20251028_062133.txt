Here's a comprehensive summary of the given content: Machine learning is a subset of artificial intelligence that enables computers to learn from data without explicit programming. It's categorized into three primary types: 1. **Supervised Learning**: This type of learning involves using labeled data to train the machine, enabling it to make accurate predictions or classifications. 2. **Unsupervised Learning**: In this type, the machine learns from unlabeled data to identify patterns and relationships within the data. 3. **Reinforcement Learning**: This type of learning involves trial and error, where the machine learns through feedback on its actions. Neural networks are the building blocks of machine learning, inspired by the human brain's neural networks. They consist of interconnected nodes (neurons) that process information and can be used for various tasks. **Deep Learning** is a subset of machine learning that uses neural networks with multiple layers to analyze and learn from complex patterns in large datasets. Its applications include: - **Image Recognition**: identifying objects or scenes in images - **Natural Language Processing**: understanding and generating human language - **Speech Recognition**: converting spoken language into text - **Autonomous Vehicles**: enabling self-driving cars to navigate and make decisions based on data.
